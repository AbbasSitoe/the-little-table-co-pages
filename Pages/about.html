ABOUT US

Creating Beautiful Celebrations For Little Moments

Creating intimate, beautiful, and wholesome food spaces designed specifically for children's celebrations.

OUR STORY

Our Humble Beginnings

The Little Table Co emerged from a desire to elevate kids celebrations away from chaotic plastic settings towards peaceful, sustainable, and highly personalized experiences.

We noticed that adults loved gorgeous styling, and children deserved spaces scaled perfectly to their height, imagination, and dietary requirements.

OUR MISSION

Making Celebrations Stress-Free

To make children's celebrations memorable and stress-free by providing creative, well-organised and affordable event packages tailored to different themes, ages and budgets.

OUR VISION

Creating Lasting Memories

To become the premier trusted children's boutique event company known for zero-fuss curation, bespoke timber setups, organic kid-approved dining, and lasting magical childhood memories.

WHO WE SERVE

Designed Around Families

Our main audience is parents and guardians planning children's birthday parties and small celebrations. We also serve families looking for themed event packages that bring catering, decorations and entertainment together.

OUR APPROACH

The Safe and Gentle Approach

Safety is our non-negotiable benchmark. All wooden surfaces are hand-sanded and splinter-checked prior to every deploy. Tableware is completely kid-safe and food-grade. Our ingredients are strictly locally sourced and allergen conscious.
